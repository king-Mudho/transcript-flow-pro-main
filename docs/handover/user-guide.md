# User guide

## Graduates

1. **Start a request** on the home page. Four steps: your details, clearance
   (tick the three offices; adding a screenshot of the portal is recommended),
   documents and delivery, then check and submit. Answers are kept on the phone
   until you submit, so a dropped connection loses nothing.
2. **Keep the reference** shown at the end: copy it, download the confirmation
   PDF or save it to WhatsApp.
3. **Track** at *Track a request* with the reference and registration number.
   Lost the reference? Use *Lost your reference number* with your registration
   and mobile numbers.
4. **Pay in cash**: to the driver in Harare, or the deposit at our hub before
   your document goes to Zimpost.

## Operations team

Sign in at `/auth` (Staff sign in in the footer). The first time, set up
two-step sign-in with an authenticator app. You are signed out after 30 minutes
without activity.

| Task | Where |
| --- | --- |
| See what needs doing | **Overview**: counts per stage, stuck requests, cash still out. The bell lists what needs attention. |
| Find a request | **Ctrl K** from any page: reference, name, reg number or the last digits of a phone. Scanning a label opens the request. |
| Work the queue | **Requests**: one tab per stage. Select rows; the navy bar offers only the moves those rows allow. Save a filtered view for later. |
| Check clearance | Open the request, view the proof, switch **Verified** on. Submitting to MSU without it needs an override note. |
| Hand requests to MSU | Select Received requests › *Move to Submitted to MSU*. A hand-in list (HL-…) is created; print it from **MSU and collections › Hand-in lists** for MSU to sign. |
| MSU releases documents | **MSU and collections › Waiting at MSU** › *Mark ready at MSU* (or MSU does it in its portal). |
| Collection run | **MSU and collections › Ready at MSU**: select, *Print pick-up list*, then after the trip *Mark collected from MSU*. |
| Rejected by MSU | On the request: *Reject*, with MSU's reason. The graduate sees it and is messaged. |
| Harare delivery | **Dispatch › Harare doorstep**: tick by suburb, pick a driver who is Free, *Create batch*. On the batch page print the **Waybill** and **Labels**, share the waybill to the driver on WhatsApp. |
| Delivery results | On the batch page mark each *Delivered* or *Not delivered* (with the reason). |
| Driver hands in cash | Batch page › *Close batch*: enter the cash. A difference needs a note. Each delivered document is recorded as paid by that driver. |
| Zimpost delivery | Record the deposit on the request (*Record payment*), then **Dispatch › Zimpost branches**: select, print labels, *Dispatch to Zimpost* with the date and tracking number. |
| Wrong address | On the request: *Edit delivery details* (until dispatch). |
| Phone, WhatsApp or walk-in request | **New request** (top bar). Choose how they contacted you; send them the reference on WhatsApp at the end. |
| Message a graduate | On the request: *Send update* opens WhatsApp with the right message for their stage. |
| Payment mistake | On the payment: *Reverse*, with a reason. Payments are never deleted. |
| Delete a request (admins) | On the request, or select rows in Requests › **Delete**. Give a reason and confirm. Requests with payments recorded or out for delivery cannot be deleted; a snapshot goes in the audit log. |
| Daily cash | **Cash and payments**: expected, handed in and variance per driver for any day. |
| Driver off today | **Drivers**: switch off *Available today*. It switches back on by itself tomorrow. |

### Admins only

**Settings**: prices, Zimpost branches (CSV import), Harare suburbs and
mapping of suburbs typed as Other, team and roles (invite by email, reset
two-step sign-in, deactivate or delete), messages to graduates, stage warning days, MSU staff portal
switch, brand, data protection (retention, export or erase one graduate's
data), and the audit log. **Drivers**: add, edit, deactivate. **Reports ›
Monthly report** for MSU, as PDF or Excel.

## MSU staff

Only while an admin has switched the MSU portal on. Sign in at `/auth`; you go
straight to the release queue.

* **To process**: requests handed in. Check clearance ticks and proof, select
  the released ones, *Ready for collection*.
* **Reject** a request with a reason from the list (and a note for the
  graduate if useful). The graduate sees the reason.
* Filter by **hand-in list** and download it as a PDF.
* **Monthly report**: volume, days with MSU and rejections by reason.

You never see graduates' phone numbers or addresses.
